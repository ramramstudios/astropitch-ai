AstroPitch — Privacy Policy

Version 1.0.0 · 28 August 2026

AstroPitch does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. Everything the app does — computing planetary positions from its bundled ephemeris and synthesising the result — happens on your device, and the birth details you enter are saved only in the app's own local storage on that device.

There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server: the app makes no network request of any kind and is fully functional in airplane mode. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere, and deleting the app deletes it.

Questions: github.com/ramramstudios/astropitch-ai

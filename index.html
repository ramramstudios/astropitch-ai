How it works

Your chart, as sound

AstroPitch reads the chart as one ensemble. In Chromatic mode, each sign locks to one note in a twelve-tone chromatic scale. In Gliss mode, the pitch center of Aries is the reference A (default = 432 hz), and a planet's exact degree gives it a precise pitch. The whole zodiac makes one musical octave: each sign moves one step around a twelve-note scale.

Chart as notation

You can also read this on a musical staff. Two placements make a pair of notes; a fuller chart makes a larger chord, spread across high and low registers rather than packed into one spot.

What each part of the chart does

  • Chromatic locks a note; Gliss lets degree set pitch. In Gliss mode, a placement moves higher as it moves through its sign.
  • Element sets the timbre. Fire is bright, Earth is weighty, Air is open, and Water is fluid.
  • House sets the gesture. The same placement may be struck, sustained, muted, doubled, or allowed to swell in.
  • Modality sets articulation. Cardinal starts, fixed holds, and mutable shifts.
  • The planet sets its place in the ensemble. The lights lead; other bodies sit higher, lower, or further back in the sound. ASC and MC are directional references you click to hear, never chord tones.

Aspects and synastry

An aspect is heard when two placements sound together. The angle between them becomes an interval, so the relationship in the chart becomes a relationship in sound. Tighter aspects are given more weight.

    In Overlay, add the sky or another chart to hear synastry. The two charts stay in the same pitch space; the strongest contacts are the ones that sound, keeping the comparison clear rather than crowded.

    Start listening

    Use Input to cast a chart from birth data, or Basic when that is all you know. Click any sign, planet, or aspect to hear it. Use Bloom for the chart as a chord, Sequence to move through it, and Drone for a changing sustain.

    Shape the chart and the sound

    Designer lets you move or mute bodies without changing the chart underneath. In Settings, choose Warm for the smoother default palette or Bright for more attack and edge. Choose Chromatic for one note per sign or Gliss to let pitch move continuously through each sign.

    Private, local calculations

    Chart positions are calculated in your browser. No birth data is sent to a server. You can choose whole sign, equal, or Placidus houses; Placidus falls back to Porphyry where it has no polar solution.

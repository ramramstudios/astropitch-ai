How it works

The mapping

Aries begins at A 440, Taurus at A♯, Gemini at B, and so on up to Pisces at G♯. This lays a chromatic scale around the zodiac: twelve signs of 30° against twelve semitones. The conversion is arithmetic:

frequency = A × 2(longitude / 360)

One trip around the zodiac is one octave. A planet at 14°22′ Aries is not "an A" — it is an A raised by 29 cents, and you can hear it.

Chart as notation

The same mapping can be read on a staff. First, two placements make an aspect dyad; add a third placement and the three pitch classes can make a triad. A full chart is the larger voicing that results when every sounding body keeps its own register.

Why aspects are intervals

If 30° is a semitone, then every classical aspect is an interval, with no extra assumptions:

    The tritone falling on the opposition is a nice accident. So is the trine landing on a major third.

    How to read a placement

    A placement is one instrument assembled from several independent layers:

    • Sign + exact degree → pitch. The fundamental moves continuously with longitude in equal temperament: a degree is 3⅓ cents. Sign also supplies element and modality.
    • Element → material. Fire is bright and driven; Earth is low-order, wooden, and sub-heavy; Air is hollow, breathy, and wide; Water is detuned, drifting, and wet.
    • House → gesture. It performs that material. The 2nd is struck, 3rd speaks with a flutter, 4th is muffled and close, 5th sings, 6th is gated, 7th is doubled, 11th is an inharmonic bell, and 12th swells in before it arrives.
    • Modality → phrasing. Cardinal begins immediately, fixed sustains, mutable carries the most movement and vibrato.
    • Planetary body → register and role. It uses the same sign × house instrument but changes octave and prominence: Moon and Mars sit an octave down, Mercury and Uranus an octave up, Saturn and Pluto two octaves down. Sun, Moon, and Ascendant anchor the mix.

    Same sign, different house

    Moon in Virgo is mutable Earth in either house: a D-region, wooden, low-order sound, voiced one octave down for the Moon. In the 3rd house, its bright transient, extra noise, short contour, and brief flutter make it speak. In the 4th house, the same material starts softer and darker, with less noise and more sub, so it sounds muffled, close, and warm. The house changes articulation, spectral envelope, density, and space—not Virgo's material or the Moon's register.

    What aspects change

    Aspects do not alter an individual voice's oscillator recipe. They make two placements an interval when heard together; the aspect list above gives those intervals. During an aspect audition both bodies use the same octave, so the interval is direct. Tighter aspects are treated as more important: they rise first in the contact list and make their bodies surface more often in Drone mode.

    The astronomy

    Positions are computed in the browser, with no server and no API. The Sun and Moon follow Meeus (the Moon with its full 60-term longitude series); the planets use JPL's approximate Keplerian elements, good to well under a degree from 1800 to 2050. Houses are whole sign, equal, or Placidus by semi-arc division, falling back to Porphyry inside the polar circles where Placidus has no solution.

    The sound

    Raw Web Audio, no library. One signal graph is built once and reused: a glue compressor, soft-clip saturation, a synthetic stereo reverb whose tail darkens as it decays, a ping-pong delay with a damped feedback loop, and a limiter. Each note allocates only its own oscillators and gains, and tears them down when it finishes.
